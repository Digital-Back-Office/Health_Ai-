# Health AI - clinic information

## About us
Health AI is an independent private medical clinic in central London offering private GP appointments, health checks, physiotherapy, blood tests and travel health. All of our doctors are GMC-registered, our nurses are NMC-registered and our physiotherapists are HCPC-registered. We are registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC).

We are a private clinic: appointments are paid for by the patient or through private medical insurance. We are not an NHS GP practice and can't register patients for NHS care.

## Address and getting here
- Address: 14 Riverside Walk, London SE1 9QQ.
- Nearest Underground: London Bridge (Jubilee and Northern lines), about a 6-minute walk. Southwark (Jubilee line) is about 10 minutes away.
- Rail: London Bridge station, about 6 minutes on foot.
- Buses: routes 43, 141 and 149 stop on Borough High Street, 3 minutes away.
- Parking: we don't have our own car park. The nearest public car park is on Snowsfields, about 5 minutes away. The clinic is inside the Congestion Charge and ULEZ zones.
- Cycling: bike racks are available outside the building.

## Accessibility
- Step-free access from street level, a lift to all floors and an accessible toilet.
- A hearing loop is available at reception.
- Assistance dogs are welcome.
- If you need extra help or more time, let us know when you book.

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 8am to 7pm.
- Saturday: 9am to 1pm.
- Sunday and bank holidays: closed.
- Individual clinicians work set sessions within these hours, so not every service is available at every time.

## Contact
- Phone: 020 7946 0000 (Monday to Friday 8am to 7pm, Saturday 9am to 1pm).
- Email: hello@healthai.example. Please don't email urgent medical questions.
- Out of hours: for urgent medical help call NHS 111. In an emergency call 999.

## Payment
- We accept debit and credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We don't accept cash.
- Payment is taken at the end of the appointment.
- Laboratory fees, vaccines and medication are charged separately from the consultation fee, and the clinician will tell you the cost before going ahead.

## Private medical insurance
- We work with most major UK insurers, including Bupa, AXA Health, Aviva, Vitality and WPA.
- Please contact your insurer before your appointment to get an authorisation code, and bring it with you along with your membership number.
- If your insurer doesn't cover the full cost, any balance is payable on the day.

## Languages
Our team speaks English. If you need an interpreter, let us know when you book and we'll do our best to arrange one.
