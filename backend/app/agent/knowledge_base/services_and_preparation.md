# Health AI - services and how to prepare

## Private GP consultation (20 minutes, £95)
- For any new or ongoing health concern, with same-week appointments usually available.
- Bring a list of any medicines you take, including vitamins and supplements.
- One concern per standard appointment works best. If you have several issues to discuss, an extended consultation may be more suitable.

## Extended GP consultation (40 minutes, £165)
- For complex issues, several concerns, or a detailed review of letters and results.
- Bring any letters, results or reports you'd like to discuss.

## Well-person health check (60 minutes, £295)
- A full check-up with a GP: medical history and lifestyle review, blood pressure, height, weight and BMI, urine test and a panel of blood tests (including cholesterol, blood sugar, liver, kidney and thyroid function, and full blood count).
- Fasting: please don't eat for 8 hours beforehand. Water is fine and you should keep taking your usual medicines unless told otherwise. A morning appointment is best.
- Results are discussed with you by the GP, usually within 5 working days.

## Physiotherapy initial assessment (45 minutes, £90)
- Assessment and first treatment for back and neck pain, joint problems, sports injuries and recovery after surgery.
- You don't need a GP referral, but some insurers require one: check with your insurer.
- Wear loose, comfortable clothing (shorts are helpful for knee or hip problems) and bring any scans or reports.

## Physiotherapy follow-up (30 minutes, £70)
- Ongoing treatment after an initial assessment.
- Wear loose, comfortable clothing.

## Blood test (15 minutes, £45 plus laboratory fees)
- Our nurse takes the sample. Laboratory fees depend on the tests and are charged separately; the clinician will confirm the cost before going ahead.
- Drink plenty of water beforehand, which makes the test easier.
- Fasting is only needed if your doctor has asked for a fasting test. In that case don't eat for 10 to 12 hours before (water is fine).
- Bring the request form or details of the tests you need, if you have them.

## Travel health consultation (20 minutes, £45 plus vaccines)
- Advice on staying healthy abroad plus any vaccinations or antimalarials you need. Vaccines and medication are charged separately.
- Book at least 6 to 8 weeks before you travel, because some vaccines need more than one dose.
- Bring your travel itinerary and any previous vaccination records.

## Dental consultation (30 minutes, £75)
- A check-up and consultation with our dentist: examination of teeth and gums, advice and a treatment plan.
- X-rays, hygiene appointments and any treatment are charged separately; the dentist will explain the costs before going ahead.
- Bring a list of any medicines you take and details of previous dental treatment.
- Dental emergencies (severe swelling, bleeding that won't stop, injury to the face or mouth): call 999 or go to A&E if it's severe, or NHS 111 for urgent dental help.

## What we don't offer
- Emergency or urgent care: in an emergency call 999, and for urgent medical help call NHS 111.
- NHS services or NHS registration.
- Home visits and video appointments are not currently available.
