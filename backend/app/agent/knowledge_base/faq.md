# Health AI - frequently asked questions

**Do I need to be registered with the clinic to book?**
No. Anyone can book a private appointment. New patients just give their name, date of birth and mobile number when booking.

**How soon can I be seen?**
We usually have GP appointments available within a few days, and often the same week. The assistant can check live availability for you.

**Can I choose which doctor I see?**
Yes. You can ask for a particular clinician, and the assistant will look for times when they're available.

**Can I book for my child or another family member?**
Yes. You'll need their details (name, date of birth and a contact phone number). Children under 16 must attend with a parent or guardian.

**How much does an appointment cost?**
A private GP consultation is £95 for 20 minutes and an extended consultation is £165 for 40 minutes. Prices for all services are listed on our website and the assistant can tell you. Tests, vaccines and medication are charged separately.

**Do you accept health insurance?**
Yes, we work with most major UK insurers, including Bupa, AXA Health, Aviva, Vitality and WPA. Get an authorisation code from your insurer before your visit.

**Will I get a confirmation?**
After booking you'll see the confirmation on screen with your booking reference. Please keep the reference: you'll need it if you want to change the appointment.

**How do I change or cancel my appointment?**
Talk to the assistant, call us on 020 7946 0000, or email us. Changes are free up to 24 hours before your appointment.

**Can the assistant give me medical advice?**
No. The assistant can book appointments and answer questions about the clinic, but it can't give medical advice, interpret results or advise on medication. A clinician can help with those, or the assistant can arrange a callback.

**Is my conversation private?**
Your details are used only to manage your appointments, in line with UK GDPR. Please don't share more medical detail than is needed to book.

**Can I speak to a person?**
Of course. Ask the assistant for a callback, or call us on 020 7946 0000 during opening hours.
