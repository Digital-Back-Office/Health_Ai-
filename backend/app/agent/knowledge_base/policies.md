# Health AI - policies

## Cancellations and rescheduling
- You can cancel or reschedule an appointment free of charge up to 24 hours before it starts.
- Cancellations or changes made less than 24 hours before the appointment may be charged at 50% of the appointment fee.
- If you don't attend and don't let us know, the full fee may be charged.
- We understand emergencies happen. Our team may waive the fee at their discretion.

## Arriving late
- Please arrive 5 to 10 minutes early, especially for your first visit.
- If you arrive more than 10 minutes late, we may need to shorten your appointment or rebook it so that other patients aren't kept waiting. A late fee may apply if we have to rebook.

## Children and young people
- We see children of all ages for GP appointments.
- Patients under 16 must be booked by a parent or legal guardian, who must attend the appointment with them.
- Young people aged 16 and 17 can book and attend on their own.

## Identity
- New patients may be asked to show photo ID at their first visit.
- To protect your privacy, our receptionist (including our AI assistant) will check your surname, date of birth and phone number before discussing any existing appointments.

## Chaperones
You're welcome to ask for a chaperone for any appointment or examination. Let us know when you book or at reception.

## Prescriptions
- Our GPs can issue private prescriptions where clinically appropriate. Private prescriptions are charged by the pharmacy.
- Repeat prescription requests are handled by our clinical team: ask for a callback and a member of the team will contact you. The AI assistant can't issue or advise on prescriptions.

## Test results
- Your clinician will contact you about your results, usually within 3 to 5 working days for routine blood tests.
- Results can't be given out by the AI assistant or by reception staff. If you're waiting for results, ask for a callback.

## Fit notes, letters and referrals
- Our GPs can provide private fit notes (sick notes), medical letters and referrals to specialists where appropriate. These may carry a small administration fee, which the clinician will tell you about.

## Feedback and complaints
We welcome feedback. To make a complaint, ask to speak to the practice manager, request a callback, or email hello@healthai.example. We acknowledge complaints within 3 working days.

## Privacy and your data
- We handle your personal information in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
- Our AI assistant uses your details only to identify you and to manage your appointments. Conversations are processed by our technology providers (including ElevenLabs and Google Cloud) to provide this service.
- Please don't share more medical information with the assistant than is needed to book. Clinical questions should be discussed with a clinician.
- You can ask to speak to a member of staff instead at any time.
